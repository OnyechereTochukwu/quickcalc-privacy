QuickCalc Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how the QuickCalc Android app ("QuickCalc", "the app") handles information. The app is provided by Tochukwu Onyechere ("we", "us"). We built QuickCalc to work without accounts and without collecting personal information ourselves.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No account, sign-up or login is required.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

2. Information stored only on your device

To make the app work, the following is saved locally on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Your saved calculation history (the calculator, money tools, converters and formulas you chose to save).
  • Your settings, such as theme and accent colour.
  • The time until which the optional "ad-free" reward is active.
  • Cached currency exchange rates, so the converter can work offline.

You can delete saved history inside the app (History → Clear). Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device.

3. Internet use

Most of QuickCalc works offline. The app uses the internet to:

  • download current currency exchange rates from a public rates service (open.er-api.com). Like any internet request, this service can see your IP address;
  • load advertisements (see section 4).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

QuickCalc shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and handled by Google under its own policies, not by us.

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app will ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Some ads are optional and chosen by you, for example watching a short video to hide ads for one hour or to export your history.

5. Children

QuickCalc is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security and retention

Because the information described in section 2 stays on your device, we do not hold it. Data handled by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Your choices

  • Delete saved history in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove locally stored data.
  • Reset your advertising ID or limit ads personalisation in your device settings.
  • Use the app offline: calculators, converters, timer and formulas work without internet (ads need internet).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
